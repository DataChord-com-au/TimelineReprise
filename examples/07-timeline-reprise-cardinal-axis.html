SIMILE Timeline Reprise: Cardinal Axis

This example shows incomplete-range finishing, sparse cardinal labels, and an end-anchored count with custom labels.

  • the first band shows ordinary date markers on their own
  • the finishing bands compare drop, truncate, and extend over an unaligned quarterly range
  • the two truncate bands show the 0.4 threshold dropping the previous marker at 3.2 and keeping it at 3.6
  • the quarter-count band paints every count and labels every second marker
  • the anchor band shows an end-anchored count with a non-zero anchor value
