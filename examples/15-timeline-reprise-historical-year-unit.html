Historical Year Unit: Classical Civilisations

This example uses Timeline.HistoricalYear and Timeline.HistoricalYearUnit for a broad comparison of ancient Egypt, the Greek world, and Rome. Reprise creates the unit-aware band and runtime. Event data uses astronomical whole-year numbers: raw 0 is displayed as 1 BCE, while raw 1 is displayed as 1 CE. No JavaScript Date values are used.
