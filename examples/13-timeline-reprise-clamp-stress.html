SIMILE Timeline Reprise: Clamp Stress

This deliberately extreme case puts three synchronized bands on one Reprise center clamp. Drag any band quickly past either boundary and then reverse direction. The coarse band moves through roughly one thousand times more time per pixel than the detail band, exposing any snap feedback or trampoline effect between synchronized bands.

  • Coarse follower: 50 pixels per year.
  • Medium follower: 90 pixels per month.
  • Detail master: 30 pixels per day.

Clamp centers: 2024-01-01 through 2028-01-01. Visible band extents differ because the clamp constrains centers rather than viewport edges.
